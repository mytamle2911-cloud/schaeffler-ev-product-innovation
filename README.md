# Schaeffler E-Guard — EV Home-Charging Safety Product Concept

**Product Innovation · Customer Insights · Hardware–Software Concept · B2B2C Strategy**

> **Project type:** University team project / academic product and business proposal  
> **Project stage:** Concept / analysis; not a deployed or commercially validated solution  
> **Industry:** Automotive aftermarket & EV charging

A student-led product innovation and go-to-market proposal for E-Guard, an EV home-charging safety adapter concept for the Vietnamese aftermarket.

## Business opportunity
The project explores a double opportunity in Vietnam's EV transition: a changing aftermarket landscape for traditional automotive-parts businesses, and consumers' anxiety about prolonged home charging through residential electrical setups.

**Product question:** How might an EV aftermarket solution make home charging easier to monitor and safer to manage, while creating a credible B2B2C expansion pathway?

**E-Guard** is a proposed inline monitoring/protection adapter concept, supplemented by a mobile-app concept. It has **not been represented here as a certified, field-tested or commercial safety product**.

## 1. Problem discovery & analysis
Market and consumer analysis identifies two linked challenges: EV adoption changes the independent aftermarket, while end users want greater visibility into charging conditions. The project includes both a retailer/garage persona and an EV owner persona; details that could identify interviewees are excluded from this public case study.

## 2. Proposed solution and decision-making
## Proposed product
The concept sits between a residential power outlet and a compatible EV charger. The report envisages monitoring electrical current, voltage, temperature and leakage; applying alerts, load regulation and protective cutoff in defined conditions; and offering a mobile experience for charging status and alerts. These are **proposed technical capabilities subject to engineering, safety testing and certification**.

## 3. Evidence, validation and limits
The report supports the concept through secondary market research, customer/persona analysis and a referenced interview video. It does **not** establish that an engineering prototype passed electrical safety tests, that a device was deployed, or that a quantified product-market-fit survey was completed. Treat consumer statements and personas as research material, not verified market-wide statistics.

## 4. Strategy & implementation
The proposed **B2B2C** model uses distributors, strategic automotive-parts retailers and garages to reach EV owners. The proposal sets an aspirational **150 touchpoints** (50 authorized garages and 100 retailers) by end-2027.

**Commercial assumptions in the report:** estimated unit cost **VND 1,036,000**; distributor price **VND 2,200,000**; suggested retail range **VND 2,990,000–3,490,000**. Financial forecasts and future volume targets are **modeled scenarios**, not historical sales.

## 5. Outcomes and learnings
The output is a product and business proposal combining customer insights, a hardware/software product concept, design considerations, feasibility assumptions and go-to-market planning. This portfolio entry does not claim validated safety performance, an actual product launch or realized sales.
