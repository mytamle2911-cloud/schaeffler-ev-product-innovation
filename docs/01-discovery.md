# Problem discovery and customer segments

## Macro opportunity
The EV transition changes repair and parts demand. At the same time, residential home charging raises concerns about visibility of electrical risk, prolonged load and charging reassurance.

## Research perspectives
- **Business:** diversify aftermarket product relevance and improve end-user brand proximity.
- **Channel:** distributors, auto-parts retailers and independent garages need credible, supportable new product lines.
- **End user:** EV owners prioritize reassurance, usability and visibility of charging conditions.

## Problem statement
How can a new aftermarket offering improve the perceived and practical manageability of home-charging risks, while fitting existing automotive distribution relationships?

**Evidence limits:** Market reports, personas and examples in the source are directional; they should not be taken as independent field validation of engineering safety claims.
