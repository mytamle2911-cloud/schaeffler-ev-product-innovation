# Business model, pricing assumptions and go-to-market

## Business model
Proposed B2B2C go-to-market through distributors, parts retailers, strategic garages and end users. Plan includes training and aftermarket partner activation.

## Target network
150 envisioned touchpoints: 50 authorized garages and 100 strategic parts retailers by end-2027. This is a **target**.

## Indicative economics from proposal
| Item | Assumption |
| --- | ---: |
| Estimated unit production cost | VND 1,036,000 |
| Distributor unit price | VND 2,200,000 |
| Suggested retail price | VND 2,990,000–3,490,000 |
| Initial investment (2026 scenario) | VND 12.015bn |

The report models unit sales of 3,000 (2027), 16,000 (2028), and 24,000 (2029). These are forecasts, **not sales outcomes**. The report also contains a date mismatch in its summary cash-flow table; this repository uses the years attached to the explicitly labeled base-scenario table without silently reconciling the two.

## Proposed launch approach
Awareness of charging risk → evaluation/demonstration → partner-channel activation and onboarding. Any public demonstrations of electrical performance require actual certified test data first.

**Source:** Report §§V–VII, physical PDF pages 23–39.
